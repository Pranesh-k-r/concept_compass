LIMITATIONS AND CONCLUSION:
While advanced ML models like HistGradientBoosting easily outperformed the majority class (9.2%) and Rule-based (18.6%) baselines, they struggle to reach the 75-90% accuracy target on this specific dataset.
Reasoning: The dataset features extreme sparsity (only ~20 interaction snapshots per student) relative to the high cardinality of the target variable (20+ specific root cause classes). Predicting complex, sequential prerequisite logic from a flattened snapshot of single interactions lacks necessary temporal depth. Sequence models like DKT (Deep Knowledge Tracing) would be required, provided denser longitudinal data.
Disclaimer: These results reflect evaluation on synthetic benchmarks and do not dictate real-world pedagogical validity. The existing rule-based system's strength is absolute explainability, rather than predictive guessing.
